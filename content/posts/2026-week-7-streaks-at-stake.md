There are six games to watch this Saturday with FBS-leading streaks on the line, and here they are, ranked by what's at stake. [The full list](/cfb-streak-king/games/at-risk/) runs 614 deep.

## Indiana, 22 straight, hosting Ohio State

There's a lot of fancy streaks on this site, but the most impressive streak is the one that requires the fewest qualifications to define, and [Indiana](/cfb-streak-king/team/indiana/) has been putting up Ws for the last two calendar years. They're a long ways off from Oklahoma's all-time winning streak of 47 games (1953–1957), but right now they're breathing rarefied air amongst [all-time streaks](/cfb-streak-king/longest-win-streaks-in-college-football-history/). [Ohio State](/cfb-streak-king/game/2026-10-17/ohio-state-at-indiana/) is gonna be the toughest test in a season that could see them enter the top 10 all-time winning streaks. And to top it all off, Ohio State has beaten Indiana 30 times in a row in conference games, putting Indiana's last regular season win over the Buckeyes 38 years ago in 1988. No matter what happens in this game, a big streak is gonna break!

## Notre Dame, 16 double-digit wins in a row, at BYU

It's been over a year since [Notre Dame](/cfb-streak-king/team/notre-dame/) had a margin of victory that required fewer than 2 digits to express. That is a freaking long time to beat everyone by more than a single score; they're within one game of tying Miami's streak from its legendary '01 season, but they'd still have 6 more games to go to beat Army's WWII-era streak of 21. Notre Dame is a 10.5-point favorite [at BYU](/cfb-streak-king/game/2026-10-17/notre-dame-at-byu/), and BYU *leads the league in covering the spread at home against ranked opponents* with 7 games covering! If you don't know, 7 is a very long covering streak; the market usually gets wise or else I'd be writing this from my private island after a few well-placed bets!

## Kansas, 24 straight rivalry losses, at Kansas State

[Kansas](/cfb-streak-king/team/kansas/) hasn't won a rivalry game since November 29, 2008, a 40–37 win over Missouri in the Border War, back when there was a Border War. Since then it's 24 straight losses to Missouri, Nebraska and Kansas State, and the Sunflower Showdown has gone 31–27, 29–27 and 42–17 the last three years. The all-time list for rivalry losing streaks is TCU's 30 (1973–79), Tulane's 26 (1956–72), and then Kansas and Utah State tied at 24. [Kansas is a 3.5-point underdog in Manhattan](/cfb-streak-king/game/2026-10-17/kansas-at-kansas-state/), a 41% chance the skid ends.

## The rest of the top six

- [UMass](/cfb-streak-king/team/umass/) has lost 23 straight against MAC teams since September 2021, already the longest run anyone has had against that conference (Eastern Michigan's 21 from 1980–82 was the mark). Its last MAC win was September 28, 2019, over Akron. [At Buffalo](/cfb-streak-king/game/2026-10-17/umass-at-buffalo/) it's a 7-point underdog: 32% to end.
- [Michigan State](/cfb-streak-king/team/michigan-state/) has lost 12 straight to ranked opponents by 10 or more, every one since November 2022, including a 49–0 at Michigan and a 42–0 at Penn State. No. 25 Northwestern [visits as a 7-point favorite](/cfb-streak-king/game/2026-10-17/northwestern-at-michigan-state/), so this one is 57% to end, and a loss by single digits ends it the same as a win.
- Two for the trivia night: [Georgia](/cfb-streak-king/team/georgia/) has beaten 50 straight animal mascots, and Auburn's Tigers visit as 19.5-point underdogs; [Oklahoma](/cfb-streak-king/team/oklahoma/) has won 29 straight at home against first-year head coaches, and Kentucky's comes to Norman.

Lines are the numbers as of Friday night. The [schedule page](/cfb-streak-king/games/) shows every game with the streak most on the line in it, and Sunday's post will say which of these actually broke.
