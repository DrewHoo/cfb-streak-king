## Firsts

- Jon Sumrall is officially the only first-year head coach to ever bring an unranked team into Austin and beat a ranked Texas.
- Kirby Smart had never lost in Athens to a team with a cat for a mascot, and had never lost to an unranked cat team anywhere, until the Auburn Tigers ended both between the hedges last night.
- Marcus Freeman was undefeated at [Notre Dame](/cfb-streak-king/team/notre-dame/) against blue teams, [16–0](/cfb-streak-king/?c=curcoach%2Cvscolor%3Ablue), until No. 8 [BYU](/cfb-streak-king/team/byu/).

## All-time streaks ended

- You might not suspect a school with a bulldog for a mascot to be so vicious against other animal mascots, but Georgia's all-time winning streak against [50 straight teams with animal mascots](/cfb-streak-king/longest-win-streaks-vs-animal-mascots-ever/) is now over.

## Notable

[Ohio State](/cfb-streak-king/team/ohio-state/) won 19–15 at [Indiana](/cfb-streak-king/team/indiana/) at noon on Saturday, ending the [longest winning streak in the country](/cfb-streak-king/longest-active-win-streaks/) at 22. For the rest of the afternoon the longest belonged to [Notre Dame](/cfb-streak-king/team/notre-dame/), at 16, until they lost at [BYU](/cfb-streak-king/team/byu/) that evening, 28–24. BYU's victory brought the league's longest winning streak back to Utah, via the [Utes](/cfb-streak-king/team/utah/)' 11-game streak, whose last loss was to, you guessed it, BYU!

- [Georgia](/cfb-streak-king/team/georgia/) had beaten 53 straight unranked teams, the longest such run in the country, until unranked [Auburn](/cfb-streak-king/team/auburn/).
- Notre Dame had won its last 16 games by 10 points or more. [Oregon State](/cfb-streak-king/team/oregon-state/) now leads the league with 5.
- [Charlotte](/cfb-streak-king/team/charlotte/) ended their losing streak--the longest losing streak in FBS--with their victory over Temple as an _18.5-point underdog_. [Louisiana–Monroe](/cfb-streak-king/team/louisiana-monroe/) now leads the league in consecutive losses at 14, after a 24–0 loss to Louisiana Tech.
- [Arkansas](/cfb-streak-king/team/arkansas/) won an SEC game for the first time since October 2024, 31–21 at Vanderbilt, ending 14 straight conference losses. It is Ryan Silverfield's first.
