## Notable

[Ohio State](/cfb-streak-king/team/ohio-state/) won 19–15 at [Indiana](/cfb-streak-king/team/indiana/) at noon on Saturday, ending the [longest winning streak in the country](/cfb-streak-king/longest-active-win-streaks/) at 22. For the rest of the afternoon the longest belonged to [Notre Dame](/cfb-streak-king/team/notre-dame/), at 16. Notre Dame lost at [BYU](/cfb-streak-king/team/byu/) that evening, 28–24. That left it with [Utah](/cfb-streak-king/team/utah/), at 11. The Utes' last loss had come at BYU, a year ago this weekend. They made it 12 at Colorado that night.

- Ohio State also left Bloomington with Indiana's [Big Ten winning streak](/cfb-streak-king/longest-conference-win-streaks/). Indiana had won 13 straight conference games and Ohio State 12. Ohio State now has 13.
- Indiana's 19-game home winning streak, also the country's longest, ended in the same game.
- [Georgia](/cfb-streak-king/team/georgia/) had beaten 53 straight unranked teams, the longest such run in the country, until unranked [Auburn](/cfb-streak-king/team/auburn/).
- Notre Dame had won its last 16 games by 10 points or more. The [longest such run left in FBS](/cfb-streak-king/longest-double-digit-win-streaks/) is [Oregon State](/cfb-streak-king/team/oregon-state/)'s, at five.
- [Charlotte](/cfb-streak-king/team/charlotte/) had lost 15 straight, the longest losing streak in FBS. It won 38–31 at Temple as an 18.5-point underdog. A year ago this weekend, Temple beat Charlotte 49–14. It is Tim Albin's first win over an FBS team as Charlotte's coach. [The longest losing streak](/cfb-streak-king/longest-active-losing-streaks/) now belongs to [Louisiana–Monroe](/cfb-streak-king/team/louisiana-monroe/), at 14, after a 24–0 loss to Louisiana Tech.
- [Ball State](/cfb-streak-king/team/ball-state/) had lost 14 straight road games, tied with Stanford for the longest such run. It won 32–31 at Bowling Green. Mike Uremovich had lost his first 11 road games as Ball State's coach. [Stanford](/cfb-streak-king/team/stanford/) [has the record alone](/cfb-streak-king/longest-road-losing-streaks/).
- [Arkansas](/cfb-streak-king/team/arkansas/) won an SEC game for the first time since October 2024, 31–21 at Vanderbilt, ending 14 straight conference losses. It is Ryan Silverfield's first.
- Will Stein, in his first season at [Kentucky](/cfb-streak-king/team/kentucky/), is the first first-year head coach to win in Norman since Les Miles of Oklahoma State in 2001. [Oklahoma had beaten the 29 since](/cfb-streak-king/?c=home%2Cvsnewcoach), one short of Texas A&M's record.

## Firsts

- [Florida](/cfb-streak-king/team/florida/) won 27–24 at No. 7 [Texas](/cfb-streak-king/team/texas/). Jon Sumrall is the only first-year head coach to ever bring an unranked team into Austin and beat a ranked Texas.
- Kirby Smart had never lost in Athens to a team with a cat for a mascot, and had never lost to an unranked cat team anywhere, until the Auburn Tigers ended both between the hedges last night. [Fifteen cats had come to Athens](/cfb-streak-king/?c=home%2Ccurcoach%2Cvsmascot%3Acat) in his 11 seasons, and [20 unranked ones had played him](/cfb-streak-king/?c=unranked%2Ccurcoach%2Cvsmascot%3Acat).
- Marcus Freeman was undefeated at [Notre Dame](/cfb-streak-king/team/notre-dame/) against blue teams, [16–0](/cfb-streak-king/?c=curcoach%2Cvscolor%3Ablue), until No. 8 [BYU](/cfb-streak-king/team/byu/).

## All-time streaks ended

- Auburn's win ended Georgia's [nine straight wins over Auburn](/cfb-streak-king/?c=vsteam%3Aauburn), dating to the 2017 SEC Championship Game, and its [50 straight wins over teams with animal mascots](/cfb-streak-king/longest-win-streaks-vs-animal-mascots-ever/), a run that began in November 2020 and stands alone as the longest of its kind.
- Notre Dame's 16-game winning streak ended at BYU, and with it [24 straight double-digit wins as the favorite after a win](/cfb-streak-king/?c=fav%2Cafterwin&dir=W10), since October 2024, the longest ever.
